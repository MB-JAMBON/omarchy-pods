# Air Pods Pro model

Modifications on 2026-09-12: geometry simplified from 70,198 to 36,834 triangles,
textures resized to at most 512 pixels and encoded as WebP, named parts,
animated lid hinge, earbud lift and presentation rotation. Converted to Qt Quick
3D assets using Qt 6 balsam. Added an external animationFrame property and
removed the generated infinite TimelineAnimation so the card controls playback.
The procedural studio.hdr lighting environment is original plugin material.

The sequence lasts 8.5 seconds: lid opening at 0.3–1.5 seconds, staggered earbud
lift at 1.2–2.75 seconds, then one complete turn at 3.2–8.5 seconds.
The model represents AirPods Pro; its geometry is not certified as Pro 3.
